About Spelvi

Spelvi is a calm, focused language-learning app built around four ideas: a smart dictionary you actually want to open, lessons that respect your time, books and audiobooks for full immersion, and progress you can feel.

The name

“Spelvi” is Swedish for we play — spel (game, play) + vi (we).

Why a Swedish word? Because language learning works best as play, not drill — and because the Scandinavian languages have a clean, frictionless quality that fits the tone we wanted for the app. Babbel did it with German, Memrise with English; we did it with Swedish.

What's inside

A dictionary that thinks

Lookups search several trusted sources at once — definitions, idioms, synonyms, and translations — and merge them into one clean answer. Definitions, pronunciation, etymology, examples, and audio in one screen.

Lessons that stick

Ten study techniques (image association, listen-and-repeat, spaced flashcards, dictation, etc.) paired with twelve grammar exercise types drawn from a large bank of real example sentences across nine languages. Grammar is graded by CEFR level, from A1 all the way to C2, so practice meets you where you are.

AI when you're stuck

Ask for a word explained in the exact context you found it, turn a set of words you're learning into a short readable story, or have a grammar mistake unpacked in plain language. AI is there when you need it, never in the way.

Speak and be heard

Practice pronunciation out loud and get scored on how close you are — so speaking, usually the part people skip, becomes something you actually do.

Read everything

Tens of thousands of public-domain books with one-tap word lookup and matching audiobook narrations, plus a built-in news reader that brings real-world articles into the same lookup experience. Read, listen, mark up, repeat.

Progress you can see

Streaks, daily goals, weekly bar charts, and per-track breakdowns — visible enough to motivate, quiet enough not to nag. Sign in and your progress is backed up to the cloud for free and follows you across devices.

Built on open foundations

Spelvi stands on an enormous amount of openly-licensed work — public-domain literature, open dictionaries and example sentences, and open-source software. We're grateful to the communities behind it. Full credits and licenses are listed inside the app.
