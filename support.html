Getting started

Where do I download Spelvi?

Spelvi is in late development. Apple App Store and Google Play listings are coming soon — sign up for launch updates from the homepage and you'll be the first to know.

What languages does Spelvi support?

The app interface is available in nine languages: English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Chinese, and Hindi. The dictionary covers many more for word lookup, with deepest support for English ↔ Russian.

Is Spelvi free?

Yes — the core experience is free. We may introduce optional premium features in the future (advanced statistics, expanded book catalog, etc.), but the basics will remain free.

Using the app

How do I change my target language?

Settings → Languages → choose “Learning language” (the language you're studying) and “Translation language” (your native language).

How do I create a flashcard set?

Open Lessons → tap the “+” button → choose “New flashcard set”. You can then add words manually or import them from your search history.

The audio for a book isn't playing.

Audio comes from LibriVox, which doesn't have recordings for every Project Gutenberg book. If a book has no audio, the playback button will be disabled. Try one of the suggested books on the Reader home — those have audio confirmed.

Can I use Spelvi offline?

Mostly yes. The dictionary databases, downloaded books, audiobooks, and your flashcards all work offline once they've been fetched. Live searches (Pixabay images, online dictionary lookups) require connectivity.

Data and privacy

Where is my progress stored?

On your device only. Spelvi keeps a local SQLite database with your lessons, flashcards, progress streaks, and reading positions. We do not have a copy on our servers.

Will I lose my data after an update?

No — local data persists across app updates. Uninstalling the app will, however, remove it. We're working on optional cloud backup; until then, treat your device as the only home for the data.

How do I reset all progress?

Settings → Advanced → “Reset all data”. This is irreversible. Alternatively, delete and reinstall the app for the same effect.

What about my privacy?

See the Privacy Policy for the full picture. Short version: your lessons stay on your device, we collect anonymous crash diagnostics for stability, and we never sell your data.

Reporting issues

I found a bug.

Send a description to support@spelvi.com. If you can include: device model, OS version, app version (Settings → About — bottom of the screen), and steps to reproduce — we can fix it much faster.

I want to suggest a feature.

Same email. We read every message and prioritize based on demand.

I'm a researcher / journalist / educator interested in Spelvi.

Drop us a line at hello@spelvi.com — we're happy to talk about the project.
