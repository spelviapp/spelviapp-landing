Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 26, 2026

Spelvi is a language-learning mobile app. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and how it is handled. Short version: your lessons and progress are stored on your device and the app works fully without an account. If you choose to sign in, that data is also backed up to our servers so it can sync across your devices. A few features (pronunciation scoring, AI help) send specific content to our servers when you use them. We use crash and usage diagnostics to improve the app, and we never sell your data.

1. Who we are

Spelvi is operated by Maxim Bekmetov. Contact: hello@spelvi.com. Privacy questions: privacy@spelvi.com.

2. Data we collect

2.1 Data stored on your device

  • Words and phrases you save to flashcards, lessons, and folders.
  • Search history (you can clear it from Settings at any time).
  • Reading progress, audio playback positions, study streaks.
  • Learning preferences: target language, translation language, theme, daily goal.

This is stored locally in your device's app sandbox (SQLite database, SharedPreferences, audio cache). The app is fully usable without an account, in which case this data never leaves your device, and uninstalling removes it. If you sign in, it is also synced as described in 2.3.

2.2 Account data (only if you sign in)

Signing in is optional and only needed for cloud backup, syncing across devices, referrals, and shared lists. We support Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In. When you sign in we receive and store, on our servers:

  • Your email address (with Apple you may choose a private relay address that hides your real one) and display name, as provided by Apple or Google.
  • A stable provider identifier used to recognise your account on return.
  • An account record (a random internal id, sign-up date, last-seen date) and, if you take part, a referral code and any active bonus period.

We do not receive your Apple or Google password. You can delete your account and all of the above at any time (see section 6).

2.3 Cloud sync & backup (only if you sign in)

When you are signed in, the learning data in 2.1 is backed up to our servers and synced to your other signed-in devices. Your device stays the source of truth; we store your records so they can be restored or merged. Cloud backup is a free account benefit and does not require a subscription. The data is stored on our own servers located in the EU (Estonia); it is not in Firebase or any third party.

2.4 AI features (only when you use them)

Optional AI features send specific content to our server, which forwards it to third-party large-language-model providers (Groq and Mistral AI) to generate a response:

  • Explain a word in context — the word and the sentence it appears in.
  • Generate a story — the list of words you ask to practise.
  • Explain a grammar mistake — the exercise sentence involved.

We send only what is needed to produce the result, not your identity. We do not use this content to train models; our server may cache a generated explanation so the same lookup is faster next time.

2.5 Pronunciation scoring (only when you use it)

When you use the speaking / pronunciation feature, the short audio clip you record is sent to our pronunciation-assessment service to be scored against the reference text. The audio is processed transiently to compute the score and is not stored after the response is returned. (This is separate from dictation exercises, where speech is recognised on-device by the operating system and is not sent to us — see section 8.)

2.6 Diagnostic data

Crash & usage analytics — Google Firebase

  • Crashlytics: stack trace of the crash, OS version, device model, app version, and an anonymous installation ID. No name, email, IP address, or the content of your study sessions.
  • Analytics & Performance: anonymous, aggregated usage and performance events (e.g. which screens are opened, app load times) so we can see which features need work. Not tied to your account identity.
  • Disabled in debug/test builds.

Server error diagnostics — Sentry

  • When our backend services (api.spelvi.com, cdn.spelvi.com) hit an unhandled error, an error report is sent to Sentry.
  • Reports include: error timestamp, stack trace, request URL path, and a server hostname tag.
  • We disable collection of request bodies and user IP addresses (send_default_pii=False in our Sentry configuration).

2.7 Network requests we route on your behalf

Some features proxy upstream APIs through our infrastructure so the app does not have to bundle third-party API keys. We do not log the contents of these requests.

  • Pixabay image search — your search query is forwarded to Pixabay through our server.
  • Project Gutenberg / Gutendex — public-domain book catalog lookup.
  • Tatoeba — public sentence corpus for grammar exercises.
  • LibriVox / Internet Archive — public-domain audiobook delivery.
  • News reader — public RSS/news articles fetched through our server.

2.8 Purchases

If you buy a Spelvi Pro subscription, the purchase is processed by Apple or Google and managed through Adapty, our subscription-infrastructure provider, which tells the app whether your subscription is active. Payment card details are handled by Apple/Google and are never seen by us. Shared word lists you create are public by design (see section 4).

3. How we use the data

  • To provide language-learning features (dictionary, lessons, books, audio, grammar, AI help, pronunciation).
  • To back up and sync your progress across your devices when you sign in.
  • To manage subscriptions, referrals, and shared lists.
  • To diagnose crashes and server errors, and to understand aggregate, anonymous usage so we can fix and improve things.

We do not: sell your data, share it with advertisers, use it to track you across other apps or websites, or build profiles for ad targeting.

5. Data retention

  • Local app data (lessons, progress, history): stored on your device, removed when you uninstall.
  • Account & synced data: kept on our servers while your account exists, and deleted when you delete your account (see section 6).
  • Pronunciation audio: not retained — processed only to compute the score.
  • AI content: a generated explanation may be cached on our server; the submitted text is processed transiently to produce it.
  • Crashlytics / Analytics: retained per Google's policy (Crashlytics typically 90 days).
  • Sentry events: 30 days on the free tier we use.

6. Your rights & account deletion (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA)

You can delete your account directly in the app (Settings → Delete account). This permanently removes your account record, sign-in identity, and all synced data from our servers. You can also request deletion by emailing privacy@spelvi.com from your account email; a web deletion request page is available at spelvi.com/support.html.

If you are in the European Union, EEA, United Kingdom, or California, you also have the right to access the data we hold about you, request its correction or deletion, or object to its processing. Anonymous diagnostic events are not linked to a personal identifier and typically cannot be tied back to a specific individual. To exercise these rights, email privacy@spelvi.com.

7. Children's privacy

Spelvi is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact privacy@spelvi.com and we will delete it.

8. Permissions used by the app

  • Microphone — used for two features. In dictation exercises, audio is recognised by the operating system's on-device speech recognizer and is not sent to us. In the pronunciation / speaking feature, the short clip is sent to our server to be scored and is not stored (see section 2.5).
  • Storage — to download books, audiobooks, dictionaries, and the word-image archive. Files live in the app's sandbox.
  • Network — required to fetch books, audio, images, news, grammar exercises, and to sync.
