Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 8, 2026

Spelvi is a language-learning mobile app. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and how it is handled. Short version: your lessons and progress are stored on your device, we use anonymous crash diagnostics so we can fix bugs, and we never sell your data.

1. Who we are

Spelvi is operated by Maxim Bekmetov. Contact: hello@spelvi.com. Privacy questions: privacy@spelvi.com.

2. Data we collect

2.1 Data you provide directly (stays on your device)

  • Words and phrases you save to flashcards, lessons, and folders.
  • Search history (you can clear it from Settings at any time).
  • Reading progress, audio playback positions, study streaks.
  • Learning preferences: target language, translation language, theme, daily goal.

All of the above is stored locally in your device's app sandbox (SQLite database, SharedPreferences, audio cache). It is not transmitted to us. Uninstalling the app removes it.

2.2 Diagnostic data sent to us when the app misbehaves

Crash diagnostics — Google Firebase Crashlytics

  • Stack trace of the crash, OS version, device model, app version, anonymous installation ID.
  • Crashlytics does not collect personal identifiers (no name, no email, no IP address, no content of your study sessions).
  • Disabled in debug/test builds.

Server error diagnostics — Sentry

  • When our backend services (api.spelvi.com, cdn.spelvi.com) encounter unhandled errors, an error report is sent to Sentry.
  • Reports include: error timestamp, stack trace, request URL path, server hostname tag (e.g. spelvi-api, gutendex, tatoeba-api).
  • We have explicitly disabled collection of request bodies and user IP addresses (send_default_pii=False in our Sentry SDK configuration).

2.3 Network requests we route on your behalf

Some features of Spelvi proxy upstream APIs through our infrastructure so the app does not have to bundle third-party API keys. We do not log the contents of these requests.

  • Pixabay image search — your search query is forwarded to Pixabay through our server.
  • Project Gutenberg / Gutendex — public-domain book catalog lookup.
  • Tatoeba — public sentence corpus for grammar exercises.
  • LibriVox / Internet Archive — public-domain audiobook delivery.

3. How we use the data

  • To provide language-learning features (dictionary, lessons, books, audio, grammar).
  • To diagnose crashes and server errors so we can fix them.
  • To understand aggregate, anonymous patterns of use (which features need work).

We do not: sell your data, share it with advertisers, use it to track you across other apps or websites, or build profiles for ad targeting.

5. Data retention

  • Local app data (lessons, progress, history): stored on your device only, removed when you uninstall.
  • Crashlytics reports: retained per Google's policy, typically 90 days.
  • Sentry events: 30 days on the free tier we use.

6. Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA)

If you are in the European Union, EEA, United Kingdom, or California, you have the right to access the diagnostic data we hold, request its deletion, or object to its processing. Note that diagnostic events are not linked to a personal identifier and we typically cannot tie them back to a specific individual.

To exercise these rights or ask questions, email privacy@spelvi.com.

7. Children's privacy

Spelvi is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact privacy@spelvi.com and we will delete it.

8. Permissions used by the app

  • Microphone — only when you use Speech-to-Text in dictation exercises. Audio is processed by the operating system's on-device speech recognizer and is not transmitted to us.
  • Storage — to download books, audiobooks, dictionaries, and the word-image archive. Files live in the app's sandbox.
  • Network — required to fetch books, audio, images, and grammar exercises.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the “Effective date” at the top. Continued use of Spelvi after changes constitutes acceptance.
