The short version. spelvi works for you, not advertisers. We don't sell personal data, we don't run third-party ad trackers, and pronunciation audio is processed for scoring and discarded unless you save it. Everything you create — saved words, progress — can be deleted from Settings.

1. Summary

This policy explains what information spelvi (“we”, “us”) collects when you use our apps and website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It applies to the spelvi iOS and Android apps and spelvi.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account information — your email address and authentication details, so your progress can sync across devices.
  • Learning data — saved words, lesson and grammar history, streaks, daily goals and progress charts.
  • Usage & diagnostics — anonymised app events and crash reports that help us fix bugs and improve features.
  • Device information — operating system, app version and a device identifier used for sync and security.
  • Website analytics — our marketing site uses privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics (GoatCounter) that counts page visits and referrers in aggregate, with no cookies, no personal profiles, and no cross-site tracking.

We do not collect contacts, precise location, or advertising identifiers.

3. How we use it

We use the information above only to operate and improve spelvi: to sync your progress, personalise lesson pacing against the CEFR scale, score pronunciation, provide support, and keep the service secure. We do not build advertising profiles and we do not sell or rent your personal data to anyone.

4. Voice & pronunciation

When you use pronunciation scoring, your microphone audio is processed to generate a score and feedback. This audio is not stored on our servers unless you explicitly save a recording for your own review. Saved recordings live in your account and can be deleted at any time.

5. AI features

spelvi's AI helpers (in-context explanations, story generation, mistake breakdowns) send the relevant text — for example the sentence you tapped — to a processing provider to generate a response. We minimise what is sent, do not include your identity in those requests, and do not use your private content to train third-party models.

6. Sharing & processors

We share data only with service providers who help us run spelvi, and only the minimum each needs: Apple and Google (sign-in, and billing for purchases), Adapty (subscription management), Google Firebase (crash and usage diagnostics), Sentry (server error monitoring), and Groq and Mistral AI (AI inference for the helper features). These processors are bound to protect your data and use it solely to provide their service to us. We may disclose information if required by law.

7. Storage & sync

Your data is encrypted in transit. Free cloud backup keeps your saved words and progress available across devices signed in to your account; this account data is stored on our own servers located in the EU (Estonia), not in a third-party cloud. Subscriptions are handled by the App Store or Google Play; we never see your full card details.

8. Your rights

  • Correct account details at any time.
  • Delete your account directly in the app (Settings → Delete account), which permanently removes your account and synced data from our servers.
  • Object or restrict certain processing where local law (such as GDPR or CCPA) provides those rights.

9. Children

spelvi is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will remove it.

10. Changes

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app or by email. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at hello@spelvi.com. We're a small team and we answer personally.
